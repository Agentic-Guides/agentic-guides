How do I save on streaming services?

Review your streaming subscriptions and cancel ones you rarely use. Share plans with family or friends where allowed. Choose ad-supported plans to save money. Rotate services based on what you watch.

What are the popular streaming services?

Popular services include Netflix, Hulu, Disney+, Max, and Peacock. Each offers different content and pricing. Compare plans and choose the services that provide the most value for your viewing habits.

How do I avoid streaming overlap?

Track what you watch and avoid paying for multiple services with overlapping content. Subscribe to one or two services at a time and rotate based on your needs. Take advantage of free trials.
