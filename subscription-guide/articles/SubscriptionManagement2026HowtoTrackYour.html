Why is subscription management important?

Managing your subscriptions helps you avoid unnecessary costs and keep track of recurring payments. Many people pay for subscriptions they no longer use. Tracking your subscriptions can save significant money each year.

How do I track my subscriptions?

Create a list of all your subscriptions, including streaming, apps, memberships, and services. Note the monthly or annual cost and billing date. Use subscription tracking apps or a simple spreadsheet.

What are the common subscription types?

Common subscriptions include streaming services, software, fitness memberships, meal delivery, and subscriptions boxes. Review each one to determine if you still use and value it.
