How do I save when shopping online?

Use promo codes, cashback apps, and price comparison tools. Wait for sales events and sign up for email alerts. Add items to your cart and wait for discounts. Use browser extensions that automatically apply coupons.

What are the best online shopping tools?

Use coupon browser extensions, price-tracking tools, and cashback apps. Compare prices across retailers and check for free shipping. Sign up for retailer newsletters for exclusive discounts.

How do I avoid online shopping scams?

Shop only from reputable retailers, check reviews, and avoid deals that seem too good to be true. Use secure payment methods and check return policies. Be cautious of phishing emails and fake websites.
