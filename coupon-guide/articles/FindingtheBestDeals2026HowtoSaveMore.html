How do I find the best deals?

Compare prices across stores and online, use price-tracking tools, and sign up for deal alerts. Shop during sales events like Black Friday and holiday sales. Use coupons and promo codes to stack discounts.

What is price matching?

Price matching is when a store matches a lower price offered by a competitor. Many retailers offer this policy. Bring proof of the lower price and ask the store to match it. This can save significant money.

How do I avoid overspending on deals?

Only buy items you need, set a budget, and avoid impulse purchases. Just because an item is on sale does not mean you need it. Track your spending and focus on genuine savings.
