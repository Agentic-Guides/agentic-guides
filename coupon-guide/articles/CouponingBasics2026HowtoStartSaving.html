What is couponing?

Couponing is using coupons and discounts to save money on purchases. This includes paper coupons, digital coupons, promo codes, and store rewards. Couponing can significantly reduce your grocery and shopping bills.

How do I start couponing?

Start by collecting coupons from newspapers, apps, and store websites. Organize them by category and expiration date. Combine coupons with store sales and rewards for maximum savings. Start small and build your system.

What are the best coupon sources?

Sources include manufacturer coupons, store apps, coupon websites, and cashback apps. Many stores offer digital coupons through their apps. Combine multiple sources for the best deals.
