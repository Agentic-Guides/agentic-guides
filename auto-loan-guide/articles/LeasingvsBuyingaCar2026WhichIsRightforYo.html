What is the difference between leasing and buying?

Leasing is renting a car for a set period (usually 2-3 years), with lower monthly payments but no ownership. Buying means you own the car and make payments until it is paid off. Each has different costs and benefits.

What are the pros and cons of leasing?

Leasing offers lower monthly payments, a new car every few years, and lower repair costs. However, you have mileage limits, no ownership, and may face fees for excess wear. Leasing is good if you want a new car regularly.

What are the pros and cons of buying?

Buying means you own the car, have no mileage limits, and can keep it after paying it off. However, monthly payments are higher and you are responsible for repairs. Buying is good if you plan to keep the car long-term.
