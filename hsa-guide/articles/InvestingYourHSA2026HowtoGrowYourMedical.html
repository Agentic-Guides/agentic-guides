Can I invest my HSA?

Yes, many HSA providers allow you to invest your balance in mutual funds, ETFs, and stocks once you reach a minimum balance. This lets your medical savings grow over time, similar to a retirement account.

How do I invest my HSA?

Check if your HSA provider offers investment options. Choose a diversified mix based on your risk tolerance and time horizon. Consider a strategy where you pay current medical expenses out of pocket and let your HSA grow.

What are the benefits of investing?

Investing your HSA allows your contributions to grow tax-free, potentially building significant wealth for future healthcare costs. Since HSA funds never expire, they can serve as a supplemental retirement account.
