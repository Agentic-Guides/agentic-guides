What are the HSA contribution limits for 2026?

For 2026, the HSA contribution limit is $4,300 for individuals and $8,600 for families. Those 55 and older can make an additional $1,000 catch-up contribution. These limits may be adjusted for inflation each year.

Should I max out my HSA?

Maxing out your HSA is one of the best ways to save for future medical costs and build tax-free wealth. Because of the triple tax advantage, many financial experts recommend prioritizing HSA contributions.

What happens if I contribute too much?

If you contribute more than the annual limit, you may face a 6% excise tax on the excess amount. Correct excess contributions before the tax filing deadline to avoid penalties.
