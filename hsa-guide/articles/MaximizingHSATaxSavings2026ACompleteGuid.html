How do I maximize my HSA tax savings?

Contribute the maximum amount each year, pay current medical expenses out of pocket, and invest your HSA balance. Save receipts for medical expenses to reimburse yourself later tax-free. This maximizes the triple tax advantage.

What is the 'pay out of pocket' strategy?

Instead of using HSA funds for current medical expenses, pay out of pocket and let your HSA grow tax-free. Reimburse yourself later for qualified expenses, allowing your investment to compound over time.

How does an HSA fit into retirement planning?

Because HSA funds never expire and grow tax-free, they are an excellent retirement savings tool. After age 65, you can use them for any purpose without penalty, providing flexibility and tax efficiency.
