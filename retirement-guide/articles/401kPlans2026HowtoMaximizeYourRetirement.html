What is a 401(k) plan?

A 401(k) is an employer-sponsored retirement savings plan. You contribute a portion of your pre-tax salary, and the money grows tax-deferred until retirement. Many employers offer matching contributions.

How much should I contribute?

Contribute at least enough to get your full employer match, as this is free money. The 2026 contribution limit is $23,500, with a $7,500 catch-up for those 50 and older. Increase your contributions as your income grows.

How do I invest my 401(k)?

Choose a diversified mix of investments based on your age and risk tolerance. Target-date funds automatically adjust your allocation as you approach retirement. Review your investments periodically and rebalance as needed.
