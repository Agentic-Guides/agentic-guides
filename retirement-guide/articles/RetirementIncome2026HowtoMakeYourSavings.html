How much can I withdraw from my retirement savings?

A common guideline is the 4% rule, which suggests withdrawing 4% of your portfolio in the first year and adjusting for inflation. This is designed to make your savings last 30 years. Adjust based on your situation and market conditions.

What income sources will I have in retirement?

Retirement income typically comes from Social Security, pensions, retirement accounts (401(k), IRA), and personal savings. Diversifying your income sources can provide stability and reduce risk.

How do I manage withdrawals?

Create a withdrawal plan that balances your needs with the longevity of your savings. Consider required minimum distributions (RMDs) from traditional accounts starting at age 73. Review your plan annually and adjust as needed.
