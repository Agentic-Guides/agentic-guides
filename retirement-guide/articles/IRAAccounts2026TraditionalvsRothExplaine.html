What is the difference between traditional and Roth IRAs?

Traditional IRAs allow tax-deductible contributions, and withdrawals are taxed in retirement. Roth IRAs use after-tax contributions, and qualified withdrawals are tax-free. Your choice depends on your current and future tax situation.

Which is right for me?

Choose a traditional IRA if you expect to be in a lower tax bracket in retirement. Choose a Roth IRA if you expect to be in a higher bracket or want tax-free withdrawals. Consider your current tax rate and future plans.

What are the contribution limits?

The 2026 IRA contribution limit is $7,000, with a $1,000 catch-up for those 50 and older. You can contribute to both a traditional and Roth IRA, but the combined limit applies.
