What auto insurance coverage do I need?

Most states require liability insurance, which covers damage you cause to others. You can also add collision, comprehensive, uninsured motorist, and medical payments coverage. Consider your vehicle's value and your financial situation when choosing coverage.

How can I get the best rates?

Compare quotes from multiple insurers, maintain a good driving record, and bundle policies. Ask about discounts for safe driving, multiple vehicles, and good grades. Consider raising your deductible to lower your premium.

What factors affect my rate?

Your driving record, age, location, vehicle type, and credit score can affect your auto insurance rate. Shop around annually to ensure you are getting the best rate for your situation.
