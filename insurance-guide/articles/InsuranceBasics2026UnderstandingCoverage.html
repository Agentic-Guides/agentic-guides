What is insurance and why do I need it?

Insurance is a contract that protects you financially against unexpected losses. You pay a premium, and the insurer covers covered losses. Insurance provides financial security for health, life, property, and liability risks.

What are premiums and deductibles?

A premium is the amount you pay for coverage, usually monthly or annually. A deductible is the amount you pay out of pocket before insurance covers the rest. Generally, a higher deductible means a lower premium.

How do I choose the right policy?

Assess your risks and needs, compare policies from multiple insurers, and read the fine print. Consider coverage limits, exclusions, and costs. Consult an insurance agent or broker for complex needs.
