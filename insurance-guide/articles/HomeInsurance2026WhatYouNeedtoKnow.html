What does home insurance cover?

Home insurance typically covers damage to your home and belongings from events like fire, theft, and storms. It also provides liability coverage if someone is injured on your property. Standard policies cover the structure, personal property, and additional living expenses.

How much home insurance do I need?

You should have enough coverage to rebuild your home and replace your belongings. Consider the cost of rebuilding, not the market value. Review your policy annually and update it as your home and possessions change.

How can I save on home insurance?

Bundle home and auto insurance, increase your deductible, and improve home safety (e.g., smoke detectors, security systems). Ask about discounts for loyalty, claims-free history, and home improvements.
