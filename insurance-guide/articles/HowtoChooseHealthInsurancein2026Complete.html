What types of health insurance plans are available?

Common health insurance plan types include HMO, PPO, EPO, and POS plans. HMO plans require you to choose a primary care physician and get referrals. PPO plans offer more flexibility to see specialists without referrals. EPO plans combine features of both. Each has different costs and coverage.

How do I choose the right plan?

Consider your health needs, budget, and preferred doctors. Compare premiums, deductibles, copays, and out-of-pocket maximums. Check whether your doctors are in-network. Use the Health Insurance Marketplace to compare plans and see if you qualify for subsidies.

When can I enroll?

Open Enrollment for 2026 coverage typically runs from November to mid-January. You can also enroll outside this period if you have a qualifying life event, such as marriage, birth of a child, or loss of other coverage.
