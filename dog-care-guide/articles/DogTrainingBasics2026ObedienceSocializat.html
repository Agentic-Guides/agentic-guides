Training your dog builds a strong bond and prevents behavioral issues. Start with basic commands and socialize your puppy early.

Basic Commands

Teach sit, stay, come, and down using positive reinforcement. Reward good behavior with treats and praise, and be consistent.

Socialization

Expose your puppy to different people, animals, and environments during the critical early weeks to prevent fear and aggression.

Behavioral Problems

Address barking, chewing, and jumping with patience and consistent training. Consult a professional trainer for persistent issues.
