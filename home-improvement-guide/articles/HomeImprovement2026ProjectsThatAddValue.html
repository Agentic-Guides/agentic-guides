What home improvements add the most value?

Kitchen and bathroom remodels, energy-efficient upgrades, and curb appeal improvements typically add the most value. A new front door, garage door, or siding can offer a high return on investment. Choose projects that appeal to buyers.

How do I plan a home improvement project?

Set a budget, research costs, and prioritize projects based on your goals. Get multiple quotes from contractors and check references. Plan for unexpected costs and timelines.

Should I hire a contractor or do it myself?

DIY can save money on simple projects if you have the skills. Hire a contractor for complex projects that require permits, specialized skills, or significant risk. Get written contracts and warranties.
