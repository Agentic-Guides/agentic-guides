What are my financing options?

Options include home equity loans, home equity lines of credit (HELOCs), cash-out refinancing, and personal loans. Government programs like FHA 203(k) loans are designed for renovations. Choose based on your equity and needs.

What is a home equity loan?

A home equity loan lets you borrow against the equity in your home, with a fixed rate and term. It provides a lump sum for renovations. A HELOC works like a credit line you can draw from as needed.

How do I choose the right financing?

Compare interest rates, terms, and fees. Consider the amount you need and your repayment ability. Home equity options often offer lower rates but use your home as collateral. Consult a financial advisor for guidance.
