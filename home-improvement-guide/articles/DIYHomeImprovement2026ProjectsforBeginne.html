What DIY projects are good for beginners?

Beginner-friendly projects include painting, replacing fixtures, installing shelving, and updating hardware. These projects are affordable, low-risk, and can improve your home's appearance. Start with small projects to build confidence.

What tools do I need?

Essential tools include a hammer, screwdriver set, drill, measuring tape, level, and basic safety gear. Invest in quality tools that will last. Learn to use tools safely and properly.

How do I know if a project is DIY-friendly?

Consider the complexity, risk, and required skills. Simple cosmetic projects are often DIY-friendly, while electrical, plumbing, and structural work should be left to professionals. Know your limits and hire help when needed.
