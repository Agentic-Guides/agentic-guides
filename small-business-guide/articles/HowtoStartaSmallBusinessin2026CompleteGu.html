What are the first steps to start a business?

Start by validating your business idea and creating a business plan. Choose a business structure (sole proprietorship, LLC, corporation), register your business, and obtain any necessary licenses and permits. Open a business bank account and set up accounting.

How do I choose a business structure?

Consider liability, taxes, and management. A sole proprietorship is simple but offers no liability protection. An LLC offers liability protection with flexible taxes. A corporation offers the most protection but has more formal requirements.

What do I need to launch?

Develop your product or service, set up your website and marketing, and establish your brand. Secure funding if needed. Launch and start building your customer base. Continuously improve based on feedback.
