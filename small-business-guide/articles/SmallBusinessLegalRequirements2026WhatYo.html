What legal requirements apply to small businesses?

Small businesses must register with the appropriate authorities, obtain licenses and permits, pay taxes, and comply with employment and safety laws. Requirements vary by state and industry. Consult a professional for specific guidance.

What licenses and permits do I need?

Requirements vary by location and industry. You may need a general business license, professional licenses, health permits, and zoning approvals. Check with your state and local government for specific requirements.

What are my tax obligations?

You must pay federal, state, and local taxes, including income tax, self-employment tax, and possibly sales tax. Register for an Employer Identification Number (EIN) and keep accurate records. Consider working with an accountant.
