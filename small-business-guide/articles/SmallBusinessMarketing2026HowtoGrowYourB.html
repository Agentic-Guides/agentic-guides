What marketing strategies work for small businesses?

Effective strategies include local SEO, social media marketing, email marketing, content marketing, and word-of-mouth referrals. Focus on reaching your target audience where they spend time online and offline.

How do I build an online presence?

Create a professional website, optimize it for search engines, and claim your business on Google. Use social media to engage with customers and share content. Build an email list to nurture leads and repeat customers.

How do I measure marketing success?

Track key metrics like website traffic, leads, conversion rates, and customer acquisition cost. Use tools like Google Analytics. Adjust your strategy based on what works and what does not.
