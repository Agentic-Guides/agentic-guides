# 5 Things to Know Before You Start With Crypto

**The 3-minute read that saves you from the most common beginner mistakes.**

---

So you're curious about crypto. Maybe you've heard about AI agents paying with USDC. Maybe a friend made money. Maybe you just don't want to be left behind.

Before you do anything, here are the 5 things I wish someone had told me. No hype. Just the honest basics.

---

## 1. Crypto is not "get rich quick"

The biggest lie in crypto is that it's a fast way to get rich.

The reality: **most people who try to trade crypto lose money.** Studies show the vast majority of traders don't beat simply holding a major coin like Bitcoin over time.

If someone promises you "10x returns" or "guaranteed profits," they're either lying or trying to scam you. Real investing is slow, boring, and requires patience.

---

## 2. Only invest what you can afford to lose

This is the single most important rule.

Crypto is **volatile**. Prices can drop 50% in a month. You could lose your entire investment.

So only put in money that, if it disappeared tomorrow, wouldn't change your life. Never borrow money to buy crypto. Never use money you need for rent or bills.

**If you can't afford to lose it, don't invest it.**

---

## 3. Start with the boring stuff: Bitcoin and stablecoins

You don't need to buy some obscure coin to get started. In fact, you shouldn't.

The safest places to start are:
- **Bitcoin (BTC)** — the original, most established crypto. Think of it as the "blue chip."
- **Stablecoins (like USDC)** — digital dollars that hold their value. The safest way to hold crypto.

Avoid the hype coins, meme coins, and "the next big thing." Most of them crash to zero.

---

## 4. Security is your responsibility

Unlike a bank, there's no one to call if your crypto gets stolen. **You are the bank.**

The basics:
- Use a **reputable, regulated exchange** (in Japan: bitFlyer, GMO Coin, Coincheck).
- Turn on **two-factor authentication** (2FA) with an authenticator app, not SMS.
- Never share your **seed phrase** (the recovery words) with anyone. Ever.
- Be suspicious of anyone who "helps" you set up a wallet or asks for your keys.

**If someone asks for your seed phrase, it's a scam. Period.**

---

## 5. Understand the taxes

In Japan, crypto profits are taxed. You'll need to report them on your tax return.

The rules can be complex and they change. **Don't ignore this** — tax problems are the most common way people get into real trouble with crypto.

Keep records of all your transactions. And when in doubt, talk to a tax professional.

---

## The 30-second summary

1. Crypto is not get-rich-quick. Most traders lose.
2. Only invest what you can afford to lose.
3. Start with Bitcoin and stablecoins, not hype coins.
4. Security is your job. Never share your seed phrase.
5. Understand the taxes. Keep records.

**You don't need to rush.** The best time to learn is before you invest, not after.
