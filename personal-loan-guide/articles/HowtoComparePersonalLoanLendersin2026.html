What should I compare between lenders?

Compare the APR, loan term, fees, and repayment options. Consider the lender's reputation, customer service, and loan amounts. Use pre-qualification to see offers without affecting your credit score.

What fees should I watch for?

Watch for origination fees, late payment fees, and prepayment penalties. Some lenders charge an origination fee of 1-8% of the loan amount. Compare the total cost, not just the monthly payment.

How do I choose the right lender?

Choose a lender that offers a competitive APR, reasonable fees, and flexible terms that fit your needs. Read reviews and check for customer complaints. Consider both traditional banks and online lenders.
