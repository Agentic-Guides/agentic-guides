What affects my personal loan rate?

Your credit score, income, debt-to-income ratio, and loan amount and term affect your rate. Lenders also consider the current economic environment. A higher credit score and lower debt generally lead to lower rates.

How can I get the best rate?

Improve your credit score, reduce your debt, and compare offers from multiple lenders. Consider getting pre-qualified, which does not affect your credit score. Choose a shorter loan term to reduce the overall cost.

What are current rates in 2026?

Personal loan rates in 2026 vary based on credit and market conditions. Rates for borrowers with excellent credit are significantly lower than for those with poor credit. Shop around to find the best rate for your situation.
