How can I improve my credit score?

Pay your bills on time, reduce your credit card balances, and avoid opening too many new accounts. Keep your credit utilization below 30% of your available credit. These actions can gradually improve your score over time.

How long does it take to improve my score?

Improving your credit score takes time. Positive payment history can help within a few months, but significant improvements may take a year or more. Be patient and consistent with good financial habits.

What should I avoid?

Avoid missing payments, maxing out credit cards, and applying for too much credit at once. These actions can lower your score. Also, be cautious of credit repair scams that promise quick fixes.
