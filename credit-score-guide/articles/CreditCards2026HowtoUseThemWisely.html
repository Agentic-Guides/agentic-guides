How do credit cards affect my credit score?

Using credit cards responsibly can help build your credit score. Pay your balance in full each month, keep your utilization low, and avoid late payments. These habits demonstrate responsible credit use.

What should I look for in a credit card?

Consider the annual fee, interest rate, rewards, and benefits. Choose a card that matches your spending habits and financial goals. Compare offers to find the best fit for your needs.

How can I avoid credit card debt?

Pay your balance in full each month, create a budget, and avoid impulse purchases. Use credit cards for planned expenses and track your spending. If you carry a balance, prioritize paying it down.
