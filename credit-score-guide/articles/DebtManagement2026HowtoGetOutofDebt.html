How do I get out of debt?

Start by listing all your debts and their interest rates. Create a budget and prioritize high-interest debt. Consider the debt snowball (paying smallest debts first) or debt avalanche (paying highest-interest debts first) methods.

What is debt consolidation?

Debt consolidation combines multiple debts into a single loan with one payment, often at a lower interest rate. This can simplify payments and reduce interest costs. Options include balance transfer cards, personal loans, and home equity loans.

When should I seek help?

If you are struggling to make payments, consider credit counseling or a debt management plan. Be cautious of debt settlement companies that charge high fees. Seek help early to avoid more serious financial problems.
