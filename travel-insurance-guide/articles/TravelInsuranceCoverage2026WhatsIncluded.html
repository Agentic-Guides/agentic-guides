What is typically covered?

Most travel insurance policies cover trip cancellation, trip interruption, emergency medical, medical evacuation, lost or delayed baggage, and travel delays. Coverage limits vary by policy and provider.

What is not covered?

Pre-existing medical conditions are often excluded unless you purchase a waiver. Routine medical care, adventure sports, and intentional acts are typically not covered. Read the policy carefully to understand exclusions.

How do I choose coverage?

Consider the cost of your trip, your destination, and your health. Choose coverage limits that protect your investment and provide adequate medical coverage. Compare policies from multiple providers.
