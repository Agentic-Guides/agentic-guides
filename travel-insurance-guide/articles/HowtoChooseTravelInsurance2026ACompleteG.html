What should I look for in a travel insurance plan?

Compare coverage, limits, deductibles, and exclusions. Check for pre-existing condition coverage, medical evacuation limits, and 24/7 emergency assistance. Read reviews and understand the claims process.

What are the key factors to consider?

Consider your trip cost, destination, age, and health. Choose medical coverage adequate for your destination. Consider add-ons like cancel-for-any-reason if you need flexibility. Balance coverage with cost.

When should I buy travel insurance?

Buy travel insurance as soon as you book your trip to maximize coverage, especially for cancel-for-any-reason benefits. Many policies have time limits for purchasing certain coverages.
