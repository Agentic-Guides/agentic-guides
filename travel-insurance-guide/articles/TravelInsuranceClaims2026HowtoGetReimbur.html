How do I file a travel insurance claim?

Contact your insurer as soon as possible, especially for medical emergencies or trip interruptions. Complete the claim form and submit supporting documents like receipts, itineraries, and medical records. Keep all documentation.

What do I need to file a claim?

You need your policy number, trip details, receipts, and documentation of the covered event. For medical claims, provide medical records and bills. For trip cancellation, provide documentation of the reason.

How long does reimbursement take?

Claims are typically processed within 30-60 days, depending on complexity. Submit all required documents promptly and respond to any requests from the insurer to avoid delays.
