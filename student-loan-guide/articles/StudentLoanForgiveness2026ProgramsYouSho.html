What student loan forgiveness programs are available?

Key programs include Public Service Loan Forgiveness (PSLF) for public service workers, income-driven repayment forgiveness after 20-25 years, and Teacher Loan Forgiveness for educators. Each has specific eligibility requirements.

What is Public Service Loan Forgiveness?

PSLF forgives the remaining balance on federal Direct Loans after 120 qualifying monthly payments while working full-time for a qualifying public service employer. This includes government and nonprofit organizations.

How do I qualify for forgiveness?

Ensure you have eligible federal loans, are on a qualifying repayment plan, and work for a qualifying employer. Submit the PSLF form annually and track your qualifying payments. Review program requirements carefully.
