What types of financial aid are available?

Financial aid includes grants, scholarships, work-study, and loans. Grants and scholarships are free money that does not need to be repaid. Work-study provides part-time jobs. Loans must be repaid with interest.

How do I find scholarships?

Search scholarship databases, check with your school's financial aid office, and look for local and niche scholarships. Apply early and often. Many scholarships have specific eligibility criteria, so tailor your applications.

How do I apply for financial aid?

Complete the FAFSA to apply for federal aid. Many states and schools also use the FAFSA for their aid programs. Some private scholarships require separate applications. Submit all required materials by the deadlines.
