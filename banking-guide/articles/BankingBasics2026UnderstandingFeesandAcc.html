What types of bank accounts are there?

Common bank accounts include checking accounts, savings accounts, money market accounts, and CDs. Checking is for everyday spending, savings is for growing money, and CDs offer higher rates for locked funds.

What fees should I watch for?

Watch for monthly maintenance fees, overdraft fees, ATM fees, and foreign transaction fees. Many banks waive fees if you maintain a minimum balance or set up direct deposit. Understand your account's fee structure.

How do I manage my banking?

Track your spending, monitor your balances, and review your statements regularly. Set up alerts for low balances and unusual activity. Use budgeting tools to manage your money effectively.
