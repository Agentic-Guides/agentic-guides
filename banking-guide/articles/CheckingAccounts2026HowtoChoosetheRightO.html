What is a checking account?

A checking account is a bank account used for everyday transactions like deposits, withdrawals, and bill payments. It provides easy access to your money through debit cards, checks, and online banking.

What should I look for in a checking account?

Consider monthly fees, minimum balance requirements, ATM access, and online banking features. Look for accounts with no monthly fees and free ATM access. Compare the features that matter most to you.

How do I avoid checking account fees?

Choose a no-fee account, maintain the required minimum balance, and use in-network ATMs. Set up direct deposit and paperless statements to qualify for fee waivers. Review your account terms regularly.
