What is online banking?

Online banking allows you to manage your accounts through a website or mobile app. You can check balances, transfer money, pay bills, and deposit checks from anywhere. It is convenient and often offers better rates.

Is online banking safe?

Online banking is generally safe when you use strong passwords, enable two-factor authentication, and avoid public Wi-Fi. Banks use encryption and fraud monitoring to protect your accounts. Monitor your accounts regularly for suspicious activity.

What are the benefits of online banking?

Online banks often offer higher interest rates and lower fees because they have lower overhead. You can access your accounts 24/7 and manage your finances conveniently. Consider a hybrid approach with a local bank for cash deposits.
