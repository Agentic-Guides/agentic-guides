What should I look for in a pet insurance plan?

Compare coverage, reimbursement rates, deductibles, and annual limits. Check for exclusions and waiting periods. Read reviews and understand the claims process. Choose a plan that fits your budget and your pet's needs.

What are the key terms?

Understand the reimbursement rate (percentage of vet bills covered), deductible (amount you pay before coverage), and annual limit (maximum payout per year). Also check the waiting period before coverage starts.

How do I compare plans?

Get quotes from multiple insurers and compare the same coverage levels. Consider the insurer's reputation and customer service. Read the policy details carefully to understand what is and is not covered.
