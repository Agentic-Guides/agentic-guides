How do I file a pet insurance claim?

Most insurers allow you to file claims online or through a mobile app. You submit the vet's invoice and medical records. The insurer reviews the claim and reimburses you, usually within a few days to a few weeks.

What do I need to file a claim?

You need the vet's itemized invoice, medical records, and your policy information. Some insurers require a claim form. Keep all receipts and records for your claim.

How does reimbursement work?

You pay the vet upfront, then submit a claim for reimbursement. The insurer applies your deductible and reimbursement rate to determine the payout. Some insurers offer direct payment to the vet.
