How much does pet insurance cost?

Pet insurance premiums vary based on your pet's species, breed, age, and location. On average, dog insurance costs $30-70 per month, while cat insurance costs $15-40 per month. Costs increase as your pet ages.

What affects the cost?

Your pet's age, breed, and health history affect the premium. The reimbursement rate, deductible, and annual limit you choose also affect cost. Higher deductibles and lower reimbursement rates lower your premium.

How can I save money?

Choose a higher deductible, a lower reimbursement rate, or a lower annual limit. Compare quotes from multiple insurers. Consider insuring your pet when it is young to lock in lower rates.
