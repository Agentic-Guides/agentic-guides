How do I start my apartment search?

Determine your budget, desired location, and must-have amenities. Use rental websites, apps, and local listings. Visit apartments in person and ask about availability, lease terms, and fees.

What should I look for in an apartment?

Consider the location, size, condition, and amenities. Check for signs of damage or maintenance issues. Ask about utilities, parking, and pet policies. Review the neighborhood for safety and convenience.

How do I get approved?

Landlords typically check your credit, income, and rental history. Have your documents ready, including proof of income and references. A good credit score and stable income improve your chances of approval.
