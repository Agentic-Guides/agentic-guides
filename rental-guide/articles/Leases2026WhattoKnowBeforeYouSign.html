What is a lease?

A lease is a legal agreement between you and the landlord that outlines the terms of your rental, including rent, duration, and rules. It protects both parties and defines your rights and responsibilities.

What should I check before signing?

Read the entire lease carefully. Check the rent amount, lease term, security deposit, and any fees. Understand the rules on pets, subletting, and early termination. Ask about anything you do not understand.

What are my rights as a tenant?

You have the right to a habitable living space, privacy, and the return of your security deposit (minus legitimate deductions). You also have the right to request repairs. Know your state's tenant laws.
