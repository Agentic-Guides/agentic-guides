What is renters insurance?

Renters insurance protects your personal belongings and provides liability coverage if someone is injured in your rental. It covers losses from events like fire, theft, and water damage. It is affordable and often required by landlords.

What does it cover?

Renters insurance covers your personal property, liability for injuries, and additional living expenses if your rental becomes uninhabitable. It does not cover the building itself, which is the landlord's responsibility.

How much does it cost?

Renters insurance is affordable, typically costing $15-30 per month. The cost depends on your coverage amount, deductible, and location. It is a small price for the financial protection it provides.
