What are my basic tenant rights?

You have the right to a safe and habitable home, privacy, and protection from discrimination. Landlords must maintain the property and return your security deposit. You have the right to request repairs and withhold rent in some cases.

What can landlords not do?

Landlords cannot discriminate based on race, religion, gender, or disability. They cannot enter your home without proper notice (except in emergencies) or retaliate against you for exercising your rights. They must follow eviction procedures.

How do I protect my rights?

Document everything, including communications with your landlord and the condition of the unit. Know your state's tenant laws. If your rights are violated, contact a tenant rights organization or legal aid.
