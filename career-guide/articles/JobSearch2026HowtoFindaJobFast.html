How do I start my job search?

Update your resume and LinkedIn profile, identify your target roles and industries, and set a daily job search routine. Use job boards, company websites, and networking to find opportunities. Tailor your applications to each job.

Where should I look for jobs?

Use online job boards like Indeed, LinkedIn, and Glassdoor. Check company career pages and professional networks. Attend job fairs and industry events. Leverage your network for referrals and recommendations.

How do I stand out as a candidate?

Tailor your resume to each job, highlight your achievements, and show enthusiasm. Prepare for interviews and follow up after applications. Build a strong online presence and demonstrate relevant skills.
