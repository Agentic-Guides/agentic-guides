How do I research a fair salary?

Use salary websites like Glassdoor, Payscale, and Salary.com. Consider your location, experience, and industry. Talk to professionals in your field. Use this research to determine a target salary range.

How do I negotiate my salary?

Wait for the employer to make an offer, then make a counteroffer based on your research. Highlight your skills and achievements. Consider the total compensation package, including benefits and bonuses. Be professional and confident.

What if the employer won't negotiate?

If the employer cannot increase the salary, negotiate other benefits like flexible hours, vacation time, or professional development. Know your worth and be willing to walk away if the offer is not fair.
