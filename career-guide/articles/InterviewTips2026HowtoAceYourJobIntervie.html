How do I prepare for an interview?

Research the company and role, review the job description, and prepare examples of your achievements. Practice common interview questions and prepare thoughtful questions to ask. Dress professionally and arrive early.

What are common interview questions?

Common questions include "Tell me about yourself," "What are your strengths and weaknesses," and "Why do you want this job?" Use the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) to structure your answers.

How do I follow up after an interview?

Send a thank-you email within 24 hours, reiterating your interest and highlighting key points. Follow up with the hiring manager if you have not heard back within a week. Stay professional and positive.
