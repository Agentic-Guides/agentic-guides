What should I include in my resume?

Include your contact information, a professional summary, work experience, education, and skills. Quantify your achievements with numbers. Tailor your resume to each job you apply for.

How do I make my resume stand out?

Use a clean, professional format and strong action verbs. Highlight measurable achievements and relevant skills. Use keywords from the job description. Keep it to one page if possible.

What mistakes should I avoid?

Avoid typos, excessive length, and irrelevant information. Avoid using generic language and unprofessional email addresses. Ensure your resume is accurate and free of errors before submitting.
